# Foundational Primary Texts to Read Critically

These are historically important, but they are **not themselves the critical framework of the course**. Students should analyze their assumptions about intelligence, humanity, progress, labor, universality, and technological power.

Bush, Vannevar. “As We May Think.” *The Atlantic Monthly* 176, no. 1 (1945): 101–108.  
[Open full text](https://www.w3.org/History/1945/vbush/)

Turing, Alan M. “Computing Machinery and Intelligence.” *Mind* 59, no. 236 (1950): 433–460.  
[Open PDF](https://www.cs.otago.ac.nz/cosc343/Resources/turing1950.pdf)

McCarthy, John. “Ascribing Mental Qualities to Machines.” 1979.  
[Open PDF](https://www-formal.stanford.edu/jmc/ascribing.pdf)

Searle, John R. “Minds, Brains, and Programs.” *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 3, no. 3 (1980): 417–457.  
[Open manuscript](https://web-archive.southampton.ac.uk/cogprints.org/7150/)

Block, Ned. “Psychologism and Behaviorism.” *The Philosophical Review* 90, no. 1 (1981): 5–43.  
[Citation and access](https://philpapers.org/rec/BLOPAB)

Engelbart, Douglas C., and William K. English. “A Research Center for Augmenting Human Intellect.” In *Proceedings of the 1968 Fall Joint Computer Conference*, 395–410. 1968.  
[Open original PDF](https://dougengelbart.org/pubs/papers/scanned-original/1968-augment-3954-A-Research-Center-for-Augmenting-Human-Intellect.pdf)

Kay, Alan, and Adele Goldberg. “Personal Dynamic Media.” *Computer* 10, no. 3 (1977): 31–41.  
[Open PDF](https://www.newmediareader.com/book_samples/nmr-26-kay.pdf)

[Open full text](https://courses.cs.umbc.edu/331/papers/eliza.html)

# Position Texts to Debate, Not Core Critical Readings

These represent influential AI-safety, techno-futurist, institutional, or professional positions. They are useful when paired with critical readings, but they should not define the course’s politics.

Russell, Stuart, Daniel Dewey, and Max Tegmark. “Research Priorities for Robust and Beneficial Artificial Intelligence.” *AI Magazine* 36, no. 4 (2015): 105–114.  
[Open PDF](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1609/aimag.v36i4.2577)

Russell, Stuart. *Human Compatible: Artificial Intelligence and the Problem of Control*. New York: Viking, 2019.  
[Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/566677/human-compatible-by-stuart-russell/)

Tegmark, Max. *Life 3.0: Being Human in the Age of Artificial Intelligence*. New York: Alfred A. Knopf, 2017.  
[Publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/530584/life-30-by-max-tegmark/)

Interaction Design Foundation. “Human–Computer Interaction,” “User Experience Design,” and “User Interface Design.”  
[Reference library](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics)

Corporate technical reports from OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta, Alphabet, and DeepSeek should similarly be treated as **primary corporate documents**, not neutral accounts of AI.
